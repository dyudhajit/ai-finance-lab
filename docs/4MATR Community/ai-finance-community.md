# 4MATR Academy - AI Finance Community

## Building a dedicated learning community focused on AI Finance

Welcome to the first step of building our own **AI Finance Community** — a public WhatsApp for finance professionals, students, analysts, engineers, founders, and curious learners who want to build the future of finance.

Our mission with the 4MATR Academy community is simple:

> Build a global community of finance professionals who experiment, build, and grow together at the intersection of Finance and Artificial Intelligence.

## Why Join the AI Finance Movement?

Finance is changing rapidly.

The traditional finance professional is no longer only expected to understand:

- Accounting
- Valuation
- Financial Modelling
- Markets

The next generation of finance professionals will also need to understand:

- AI-assisted workflows
- Automation
- Data analysis
- Software tools
- Dashboard development
- Digital finance products

The AI Finance Community will help professionals navigate this transition.
